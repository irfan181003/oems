1. Functional Requirements (FR)

The functional requirements below are derived from the Use Case Diagram for the Office Employee
Management System. They describe the services and operations the system must provide to its identified
actors. Each requirement explicitly identifies the related use case, actors, and assumptions, as required.
Requirement and use case numbers follow the labels in the diagram.

Common precondition: for every use case below, the user is authenticated and has been assigned a valid
role. Access is restricted according to that role as described in NFR-21 and NFR-22.

FR-1 — UC-01 – Mark Attendance

The system shall allow an Employee to record attendance for the applicable working period and store the
attendance entry against the employee record. The actor involved is Employee. Assumption: the employee is
authenticated and attendance marking is permitted for the relevant date or working period.

FR-2 — UC-02 – View Attendance

The system shall allow authorized users to view attendance information. An Employee can view only their own
attendance, and the HR Manager can view attendance of all employees. The attendance information is also
used by Generate Financial/Payroll Report (UC-17) through an <<include>> relationship. The actors involved
are Employee and HR Manager. Assumption: attendance data has been recorded and the requesting user has
permission to view it.

FR-3 — UC-03 – Apply Leave

The system shall allow an Employee to submit a leave application containing the required leave details for
processing. The actor involved is Employee. Assumption: the employee is authenticated, eligible to apply for
leave, and provides all required information.

FR-4 — UC-04 – Manage Leave

The system shall allow the HR Manager to review, approve or reject, update, and process submitted leave
applications and maintain the leave status. The actor involved is HR Manager. Assumption: a leave application
has been submitted through UC-03 and leave rules or approval permissions are configured.

FR-5 — UC-05 – Manage Profile

The system shall allow an Employee to view and update permitted personal profile information. The actor
involved is Employee. Assumption: the employee is authenticated and only fields permitted for self-service
editing are changed.

FR-6 — UC-06 – Register Employee

The system shall allow the HR Manager to register a new employee by entering the required employee
information and creating the corresponding employee record. The actor involved is HR Manager. Assumption:
the new employee does not already have a duplicate active record and all mandatory registration information is
available.

FR-7 — UC-07 – Manage Records

The system shall allow the HR Manager to view, update, and maintain employee records. The actor involved is
HR Manager. Assumption: the HR Manager is authenticated and the employee record being managed is within
the system's scope.

FR-9 — UC-09 – Manage Vacancies

The system shall allow the Recruitment Officer to create, view, update, and close job vacancy information. The
actor involved is Recruitment Officer. Assumption: the Recruitment Officer is authorized to manage vacancies
and provides the required vacancy details.

FR-10 — UC-10 – Schedule Interviews

The system shall allow the Recruitment Officer to create and manage interview schedules for candidates,
including relevant date, time, and interview information. The actor involved is Recruitment Officer. Assumption:
candidate information is available and the selected interview slot is valid.

FR-11 — UC-11 – View Salary

The system shall allow authorized users to view salary information relevant to their role. An Employee can view
only their own salary information, while the HR Manager, Payroll Officer, and Finance Manager can view salary
information of employees as required by their responsibilities. The actors involved are Employee, HR Manager,
Payroll Officer, and Finance Manager. Assumption: salary information exists for the employee and access
rights permit the requested view.

FR-12 — UC-12 – Calculate Salary

The system shall calculate employee salary using the configured salary information and applicable payroll
inputs maintained by the system. The calculated salary is used by Generate Payslip (UC-13), Manage Payroll
(UC-14), and Generate Financial/Payroll Report (UC-17) through an <<include>> relationship. No actor is
directly associated with this use case in the diagram. Assumption: required salary, attendance, leave, and other
applicable payroll data are accurate and available.

FR-13 — UC-13 – Generate Payslip

The system shall generate a payslip containing the applicable salary and payroll details for an employee.
Payslip generation includes the salary calculation performed by UC-12. No actor is directly associated with this
use case in the diagram. Assumption: the employee's payroll inputs and salary information have been recorded
and are available for calculation.

FR-14 — UC-14 – Manage Payroll

The system shall manage payroll information and payroll processing activities, including maintaining payroll
data and processing salary for the payroll period. Payroll processing includes the salary calculation performed
by UC-12. No actor is directly associated with this use case in the diagram. Assumption: required employee and
salary data are available and access is restricted to authorized users.

FR-15 — UC-15 – View Payroll Records

The system shall allow the Payroll Officer to view employee and payroll records required for payroll processing
and verification. The payroll records are also used by Generate Financial/Payroll Report (UC-17) through an
<<include>> relationship. The actor involved is Payroll Officer. Assumption: the requested records exist and the
Payroll Officer has appropriate access rights.

FR-16 — UC-16 – Generate HR Report

The system shall allow the HR Manager to generate human-resource reports based on available records, such
as attendance, leave, and employee information. The actor involved is HR Manager. Assumption: sufficient and
valid source data is available and the HR Manager has reporting permission.

FR-17 — UC-17 – Generate Financial/Payroll Report

The system shall allow the Finance Manager to generate financial and payroll reports based on salary and
payroll records for financial oversight. Report generation includes the attendance information provided by View
Attendance (UC-02), the salary calculation performed by Calculate Salary (UC-12), and the records provided by
View Payroll Records (UC-15). The actor involved is Finance Manager. Assumption: sufficient and valid payroll
and salary data is available and the Finance Manager has reporting permission.

FR-19 — UC-19 – Generate Offer Letter

The system shall allow the Recruitment Officer to generate an offer letter for a selected candidate, containing
the position, offer terms, and joining details. The actor involved is Recruitment Officer. Assumption: the
candidate has been selected for a vacancy and the offer details are available.

FR-20 — UC-20 – Manage Retirement Plans

The system shall allow the HR Manager to create, view, update, and maintain retirement plan information for
employees, such as retirement dates and related details. The actor involved is HR Manager. Assumption: the
employee record exists and the required retirement information is available.

2. Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)

The following non-functional requirements specify quality, security, reliability, integrity, performance, and
usability expectations associated with the functional requirements.

NFR-1 — Associated with FR-1

Attendance entries shall be recorded accurately and shall prevent duplicate or inconsistent submissions for the
same working period.

NFR-2 — Associated with FR-2

Attendance information shall be displayed accurately and within a reasonable response time. Employees shall
see only their own records, and access to other attendance records shall be limited to authorized roles.

NFR-3 — Associated with FR-3

Leave applications shall validate required fields before submission and shall preserve the submitted information
accurately.

NFR-4 — Associated with FR-4

Leave records shall remain consistent throughout review, approval, and processing. Unauthorized users shall
not be able to alter leave information, and every status change shall be traceable to the user who made it.

NFR-5 — Associated with FR-5

Profile updates shall validate input data and protect personal information from unauthorized access or
modification.

NFR-6 — Associated with FR-6

Employee registration shall validate mandatory information and prevent duplicate employee identifiers. Newly
created records shall be stored reliably.

NFR-7 — Associated with FR-7

Employee records shall maintain data integrity, and updates shall be traceable to the authorized user making
the change.

NFR-9 — Associated with FR-9

Vacancy information shall be consistently maintained, with validation of required fields and controlled access to
recruitment data.

NFR-10 — Associated with FR-10

Interview schedules shall be stored accurately and should minimize conflicting or ambiguous scheduling
information.

NFR-11 — Associated with FR-11

Salary information shall be confidential, accurate, and accessible only to authorized roles. An Employee shall
not be able to view the salary of another employee.

NFR-12 — Associated with FR-12

Salary calculations shall be consistent and reproducible for the same valid inputs. The system shall preserve
calculation accuracy and protect payroll data.

NFR-13 — Associated with FR-13

Payslips shall accurately reflect the payroll calculation data and shall be accessible only to authorized users.
Generated payslips should have a consistent and readable format.

NFR-14 — Associated with FR-14

Payroll data shall maintain integrity during creation or modification, with role-based authorization and reliable
storage of processed information.

NFR-15 — Associated with FR-15

Payroll records shall load reliably and remain confidential. The system should provide sufficient response speed
for routine payroll verification.

NFR-16 — Associated with FR-16

HR reports shall be generated from consistent data, shall include only the information the HR Manager is
permitted to access, and shall be presented in a readable format within a reasonable response time.

NFR-17 — Associated with FR-17

Financial and payroll reports shall be generated from consistent and accurate payroll data, shall be accessible
only to the Finance Manager, and shall be presented in a readable format within a reasonable response time.

NFR-19 — Associated with FR-19

Offer letters shall accurately reflect the selected candidate and offer details, shall be presented in a consistent
and readable format, and shall be accessible only to authorized recruitment users.

NFR-20 — Associated with FR-20

Retirement plan information shall be stored accurately and kept confidential, shall be modifiable only by
authorized HR users, and updates shall be traceable to the user making the change.

NFR-21 — Associated with all FRs (Role-Based Access Control)

Role-based access control shall be enforced consistently across the system so that users can perform only the
operations permitted for their assigned role. Unauthorized attempts to access restricted functions or records
shall be denied.

NFR-22 — Associated with all FRs (Authentication and Session Security)

Users shall be authenticated with valid credentials before using any function of the system. Credentials shall be
protected, and an active session shall be terminated securely on sign-out or after a period of inactivity so that
an unattended session cannot be used by another person.

3. System Scope and Actor Summary

The Office Employee Management System is intended to centralize employee-related information and
routine human-resource, recruitment, attendance, leave, payroll, salary, and reporting activities. The supplied
diagram identifies five external actors: Employee, HR Manager, Recruitment Officer, Payroll Officer, and
Finance Manager. The system supports role-specific access so that each actor can perform the operations
relevant to their responsibilities.

Employee: Marks attendance, applies for leave, manages their own profile, and views their own attendance
(UC-02) and salary (UC-11).

HR Manager: Registers employees, maintains employee records, manages leave, manages retirement plans,
views attendance and salary information, and generates HR reports.

Recruitment Officer: Manages job vacancies, schedules interviews, and generates offer letters for recruitment
activities.

Payroll Officer: Views salary information and payroll records.

Finance Manager: Views salary information and generates financial/payroll reports for financial oversight.

Payroll processing: Calculate Salary (UC-12), Generate Payslip (UC-13), and Manage Payroll (UC-14) are
not directly associated with an actor in the diagram. UC-13 and UC-14 include UC-12, which is also included by
UC-17.

4. Use Case Diagram Reference

Figure 1: Use Case Diagram for the Office Employee Management System.

Relationships shown in the diagram

The diagram contains the following <<include>> relationships. In each case, the dependent use case requires
the included use case to complete its function.

• UC-13 Generate Payslip and UC-14 Manage Payroll both depend on UC-12 Calculate Salary, because
   every payslip and every payroll run requires a salary calculation.

• UC-17 Generate Financial/Payroll Report depends on UC-02 View Attendance, UC-12 Calculate Salary,
   and UC-15 View Payroll Records, because financial and payroll reports are built from attendance, calculated
   salary, and payroll records.

All other use cases are connected directly to their actors, except UC-12, UC-13, and UC-14, which are not
directly associated with an actor in the diagram.

The Director actor, UC-08 View Organizational Records, and UC-18 Generate Organization Summary Report
do not appear in the revised diagram and have been removed. UC-19 Generate Offer Letter and UC-20
Manage Retirement Plans have been added. The remaining use cases keep the numbers shown in the
diagram.
